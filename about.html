About the MIS Society

Connecting, collaborating, and engaging.

Our mission

We help you move from student to professional

The MIS Society connects students with each other, with College of Business Administration faculty and staff, and with people who work in the industry.

Connect

Meet other students, faculty, staff, and industry professionals, and build a network that lasts.

Learn at events

Join events and seminars on career development and professional networking.

Understand the industry

Learn how the IT industry works and what role Management Information Systems plays in business today.
