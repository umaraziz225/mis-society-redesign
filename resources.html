Your CSUSM account already unlocks software that costs money elsewhere. Here is what it is and how to turn each tool on.

ChatGPT Edu

Provided by CSUSM

A ChatGPT account the CSU system pays for. Your chats are not used to train OpenAI's models.

Good for: Coursework

Usage limits apply to most features since September 1, 2026, and CSUSM cannot reset them. Basic features stay available.

Check each instructor's AI policy before using AI for coursework.

How to get it

  1. Signing up is optional. Request your license with the ChatGPT Edu License Request form on the CSUSM page.
  2. Open the invite email and select "Join Workspace". You can also go to ChatGPT and choose "Log in" (not sign-up).
  3. Click the top box that says "San Marcos" and log in with your campus username and password.
  4. Already have a ChatGPT account with your CSUSM email? You will be asked whether to move your chats. Anything you do not move is deleted.
Official CSUSM page (opens in new tab)

Last checked: November 2026

Microsoft 365 + Copilot

Provided by CSUSM

Microsoft 365 for every active CSUSM student: Office apps in your browser or installed on your own devices, plus OneDrive cloud storage.

Good for: Coursework Career

CSUSM's ChatGPT page also points to Microsoft Copilot, which can be used in most cases without usage limits.

Check each instructor's AI policy before using AI for coursework.

How to get it

  1. Open a web browser, go to Office.com, and log in with your campus username and password.
  2. To install the apps, follow the "Install on a Mac or PC" guide on the CSUSM page. Personally owned devices are allowed.
  3. Plan ahead: you lose access when you leave CSUSM, so back up your files to personal cloud storage before you graduate.
Official CSUSM page (opens in new tab)

Last checked: November 2026

Adobe Creative Cloud

Provided by CSUSM

Photoshop, Illustrator, Premiere Pro, and the rest of Adobe Creative Cloud, free while you are a currently enrolled student.

Good for: Design Career

How to get it

  1. CSUSM gives students redemption codes. Follow the install and redemption steps on the CSUSM Adobe page (it has a how-to video).
  2. Sign in to the apps to use them. You can use them on campus computers and on your home computer.
  3. You keep access as long as you are enrolled. Back up your files before you graduate.
Official CSUSM page (opens in new tab)

Last checked: November 2026

MIS-relevant software

Provided by CSUSM

Software from your MIS classes that CSUSM lists for students: SPSS, Visio, SQL Developer, SAP Logon, R and R Studio, StarUML, and Minitab.

Good for: Coursework Data Coding

How to get it

  1. Open CSUSM's campus software list.
  2. Find the program. The "Cougar Apps - Students" column shows what you can run through Cougar Apps, and "Available for Personal Devices" shows what you can download.
  3. Need software for a class that is not on the list? CSUSM says to contact your instructor.
Official CSUSM page (opens in new tab)

Last checked: November 2026

Azure for Students

Free with student email

$100 in Azure credit to use in your first 12 months, plus select free services. No credit card needed.

Good for: Coding Data

You must be 18 or older and a full-time student at an accredited, degree-granting school.

How to get it

  1. Go to Microsoft's Azure for Students page and select the apply link.
  2. Verify your student status with your school email address.
  3. Use your credit within 12 months. Unused credit does not carry over.
Official Microsoft page (opens in new tab)

Last checked: November 2026

GitHub Student Developer Pack

Free for verified students

Free GitHub Pro while you are a student, plus offers from partner companies. Verified students can also get GitHub Copilot Student.

Good for: Coding Career

Copilot Student includes unlimited code completions but only limited chat and agent use.

Check each instructor's AI policy before using AI for coursework.

How to get it

  1. Go to education.github.com/pack and select the sign-up button.
  2. Verify that you are a student when GitHub asks.
  3. Browse the pack to pick the offers you want, such as GitHub Pro and GitHub Copilot.
