Join the MIS Society

The MIS Society is a group of CSUSM students and faculty focused on the professional success of our members. If you want a career where business meets technology, you will find speakers, internship help, and a community here.

Member perks

What you get

  • Guest speakers and real Q&A. Not just presentations.
  • Internship prep. Help with your resume, LinkedIn, interviews, and recruiting strategy.
  • Career clarity. Roles explained and paths mapped, so you can choose with confidence.
  • Community. Meet people who actually show up and grow.

Three steps

How joining works

  1. Fill out the interest form or email us

    Use the form below, or write to csusmmissociety@gmail.com.

  2. Come to a meeting or event

    See the Events page and the club's LinkedIn for what is coming up.

  3. Get involved

    Attend, volunteer, or run for an officer role.
