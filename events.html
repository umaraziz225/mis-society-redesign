What to expect

Types of events we run

Our events fall into four kinds. Come to one, or come to all of them.

Guest speakers

Professionals share how they built their careers and answer your questions. It is real Q&A, not just a presentation.

Workshops

Hands-on sessions that build skills you can use in internship applications and class projects.

Company visits

Visit organizations to see how technology and MIS teams work, and meet the people on them.

Socials

Meet other members, officers, and faculty in a relaxed setting and grow your network.
