Where do you fit in Information Systems?

MIS leads to a lot of different careers. This quiz asks how you feel about real tasks, builds your work profile, and matches it with 12 career families, including each one's job outlook and how AI is changing it.

  2. Part B: 6 workplace scenarios about 3 min
